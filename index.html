PALIMPSEST

"it writes over itself."

In the winter of 1977 a radio astronomer at the decommissioned Noctua Array logged one recording, annotated once in pencil in the margin of the logbook. She was never seen again. Everyone who opens the file sees static, and every one of them swears they saw something different inside. They are all right. She wrote three times. Recover the last thing she heard.
