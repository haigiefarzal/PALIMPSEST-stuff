## Scenario

In the winter of 1977, a radio astronomer at the decommissioned Noctua Array logged a single
recording. She annotated it once, in pencil, in the **margin** of the logbook: *"it writes over
itself."* She was never seen again.

The disk resurfaced last spring at an estate sale. There is one file on it. Everyone who opens it
sees static — and every one of them swears they saw something different inside.

**They are all right.**

A palimpsest never forgets a single layer of ink. You only have to make the old writing bleed
through. She wrote **three times**. She left the method in the margin — if you can find the margin.

*Recover the last thing she heard.*
